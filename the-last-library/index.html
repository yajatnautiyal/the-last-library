Timeline

Seventy years of remembering.

How the archive went from a rescue effort to the last library on Earth.

2025

The Great Backup

Volunteers begin copying public records, books and recordings onto durable storage, just in case.

2041

The last print library closes

Funding ends for the final public lending library. Its shelves are scanned over eleven weeks.

2067

The Long Blackout

Regional networks fail for most of a year. Whatever was stored only online is lost.

2079

Node 01 is built

A sealed vault, powered and cooled by the ground around it, is completed beneath a mountain.

2095

The doors open

The Last Library welcomes its first visitors. The archive is open, and still unfinished.

About the library

We are not here to rebuild the world. We are here so that nobody has to wonder what it was like.

Keep everything

We do not decide what is worth saving. Ordinary things are often the rarest.

Show the gaps

Every record states what is missing. An honest hole is better than a guessed fill.

Open to all

No fee, no membership. Anyone can walk in or request a copy.
